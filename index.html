Probability Syndicate

Bio

Eric Salazar has been producing music for 40 years. His ‘Phidippus’ project streamed at least 3 million times online and has been lauded critically by publications such as Earmilk and Modern Magnetic.

With Probability Syndicate, Eric explores hybrid production, combining conventional production with AI tools. Every song is seeded by Eric and goes well beyond prompting and pushing a button. Each track features a distinctive voice and the backbone of each tune is Eric’s own composition and performance.

Exploring disparate genres, textures and moods Eric and the machine weave a tapestry of sonic mayhem.
